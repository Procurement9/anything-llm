# Estate Planning Pro Skill

## Overview

This skill provides a complete workflow for estate planning and tax preparation, integrating real-time financial data and regulatory filings.

## Core Modules

### 1. Asset & Liability Management
- **Inventory**: Track real estate, investments, bank accounts, and personal property.
- **Valuation**: Real-time valuation of liquid assets.
- **Liabilities**: Track mortgages, loans, and other debts.
- **Net Worth**: Automatic calculation of total estate value.

### 2. Tax Preparation & Optimization
- **Federal Estate Tax**: Calculate estimated liability based on current IRS rules (e.g., 2025/2026 thresholds).
- **Deductions**: Model funeral expenses, administration costs, charitable bequests, and marital deductions.
- **Optimization**: Strategies for minimizing tax exposure (e.g., Rule 72(t) modeling).
- **Form 706**: Preparation of data for federal estate tax returns.

### 3. Financial Terminal (Bloomberg-Style)
- **Real-time Quotes**: Live market data for stocks and ETFs.
- **Watchlists**: Monitor key assets in the estate.
- **News Feed**: Financial news relevant to estate holdings.
- **Technical Analysis**: Basic technical indicators for asset monitoring.

### 4. SEC Filings & Due Diligence
- **EDGAR Integration**: Direct access to SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K).
- **Comment Letters**: Identification of SEC correspondence (UPLOAD/CORRESP) for risk assessment.
- **Due Diligence**: Analyzing company responses to SEC inquiries.

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Data Entry & Inventory
1. Gather all asset and liability information.
2. Input data into the management module.
3. Categorize assets for tax purposes.

### Step 2: Market Analysis
1. Use the Terminal to verify current market values of liquid assets.
2. Check recent news for any impact on estate valuation.
3. Review SEC filings for significant holdings to assess risk.

### Step 3: Tax Modeling
1. Run the tax calculator with current estate values.
2. Apply all eligible deductions.
3. Review optimization suggestions to reduce projected liability.

### Step 4: Reporting & Documentation
1. Generate net worth statements.
2. Prepare tax estimation reports.
3. Export data for professional review.

## Reference Resources
- **IRS Publication 559**: Survivors, Executors, and Administrators.
- **SEC EDGAR Database**: For regulatory filings.
- **Rule 72(t)**: Guidelines for early retirement distributions.
